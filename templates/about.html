About EduLearn Hub

EduLearn Hub is a next-generation AI-powered mock preparation and online evaluation platform for competitive exams like Railway, SSC, and Banking in India.

Our platform allows students to register, attempt dynamic AI-generated quiz questions based on their weak topics, and view comprehensive performance analysis. We integrate state of the art LLM technologies (specifically Google Gemini Pro API) to generate unique sets of high quality test questions on-demand.

Our Key Objectives

  • Smart test personalization to address individual student weaknesses.
  • On-demand question bank generation with zero delay.
  • Role-based coordination between teachers, students, and system administrators.
  • Interactive analytics using visual data dashboards.
EduLearn Hub was designed by the Google DeepMind team as a demonstration of a modern evaluation platform.
